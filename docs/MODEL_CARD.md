# Model Card

## Model details

The classifier is a deterministic rule baseline. It assigns one of four operational categories from title phrases, source context and a documented precedence order. A separate alert layer uses market terms and deadline patterns. Retrieval uses a dependency-free BM25 implementation over Chinese character bigrams and ASCII tokens.

## Intended use

The system supports analysts who triage public electricity-market notices through a supervised review workflow. It is not suitable for automatic bidding, settlement approval, regulatory attestation or unattended compliance decisions.

## Evaluation

Metrics are generated by `market_radar.evaluation` over the checked-in 100-record snapshot. They include per-class precision, recall and F1; macro F1; market-impact precision, recall, F1 and specificity; alert volume; retrieval hit rate@1/@3; and latency. The labels remain `development_draft` until the project author completes row-by-row verification.

## Limitations

- Draft labels are not independent ground truth and may share assumptions with the rules.
- The sample is stratified across official source sections and cannot estimate production prevalence.
- Keyword rules can miss paraphrases and overreact to dates or operational terms used in training material.
- Retrieval questions are small and close to source wording.
- Attachment-only content and login-only disclosures are outside the snapshot.

## Human oversight

Every alert and answer exposes its supporting record and official URL. Users must open the source and verify eligibility, dates, attachments and corrections. Low-confidence and deadline-triggered items remain in a human review queue.
